Frequently Asked Questions

Lock Harbor

Can you see my passwords?

No, and not as a promise — as a matter of design. There is no Lock Harbor server and no account. Your vault is an encrypted file on your phone. We have no copy of it and no way to obtain one.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Your recovery key — shown once when you set up the app — lets you set a new master password. If you have neither the master password nor the recovery key, and no backup file, the vault cannot be opened by anyone, including us. There is no reset link, because there is nothing to send one from.

What if I lose or break my phone?

If you have an encrypted backup, install Lock Harbor on the new phone and restore from it. If you have no backup, your entries are gone. This is the direct cost of nothing being stored on a server, and it is the single best reason to take a backup today.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not yet. Your vault lives on one phone. Moving to another device means taking an encrypted backup and restoring it there. Sync is planned but deliberately last, because it is the feature that would introduce a server.

Does it work without internet?

Completely. You can use Lock Harbor in airplane mode indefinitely. The only feature that ever makes a network request is the optional breach check, which is off until you enable it.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet — Lock Harbor is Android for now. Filling passwords on iOS works through a different mechanism that has to be built separately rather than ported.

I turned on autofill but nothing appears in my browser.

Almost always the browser's own setting. Choosing Lock Harbor in Android's settings is not enough for most browsers — in Chrome you also need ⋮ → Settings → Autofill services → Autofill using another service. Firefox needs nothing extra.

Samsung Internet will not work at all yet, whatever you configure — it asks a newer part of Android that Lock Harbor does not answer yet. Use Chrome or Firefox.

A quick test: if filling works in an ordinary app but never in the browser, it is the browser.

Why does a login I have saved not get offered?

Matching runs on the website address stored against the entry. If it has none, or it does not match the site asking, it is not offered — even if the name looks right. Open the entry and add the address. Lock Harbor deliberately never offers a password it cannot tie to whoever is asking, and treats a look-alike address as a different site.

Has Lock Harbor been security audited?

No. It uses well-established, published cryptography (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305) through maintained libraries rather than anything home-made, and the protocol is documented. But no external party has reviewed it, and we will not describe it as audited or "military grade" until that is actually true.

Is the security code on my card ever scanned?

No. Card scanning reads the number, expiry and cardholder name only. The security code is always typed by you.

Are photos of my documents saved when I scan them?

No. The camera image is read for the fields and discarded immediately. Nothing is written to your photo library and nothing is uploaded.

Why did a scan get a field wrong?

A driver's licence is read from a barcode and is very reliable. Cards and passports are read from printed text, which is inherently less certain — a field can come back blank or slightly wrong. Embossed cards with raised numbers are the hardest. Always check what was filled before saving.

Why is a passport's middle name sometimes missing?

The machine-readable lines separate given names with a single character that the camera occasionally misreads or loses, which merges the names into one. It fills about two thirds of the time on a clean scan. Rescanning often works; typing it takes two seconds.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, two ways: an encrypted backup, or a plain-text CSV/JSON export for moving to another manager. The plain-text one is unprotected by definition, so it warns you and asks for your master password first.

Does it show ads or cost anything ongoing?

No ads, no trackers, and no subscription mechanism in the app.

Can I block screenshots of my vault?

Yes, but it is off by default — screenshots work normally until you choose otherwise. Turning on Settings → Privacy → Block screenshots stops screenshots, screen recording and screen sharing, and hides the app in the app switcher. Worth switching on if you are ever somewhere your screen could be captured without you noticing.
